Bhai jan, main ne note kiya hai ke aap ab "Adamjee Coaching Center" ke project par kaam kar rahay hain. Ek Coaching Center / Academy ki requirements ek normal school se bilkul mukhtalif hoti hain.

Agar aap Coaching Center ke hawale se system ko next level par le jana chahte hain, tou yeh Features add karna sab se zyada best rahay ga:

1. 🎓 Batch & Subject-Wise Enrollment System
Coaching centers mein bacha sirf "Class 10" mein enroll nahi hota, wo specific subjects (e.g., Physics Batch A, Chemistry Batch C) mein enroll hota hai.

System automatically bache ke selected subjects ki base par uski Monthly Fee calculate kare.
Har subject/batch ki alag se attendance mark ho (taake pata chale bacha Physics mein aya hai lekin Math mein absent tha).
2. 📝 Test Series & Mock Exams Tracker
Coaching centers ka main focus tests par hota hai (Weekly tests, Grand tests, Mock Exams).

Ek aesa module jahan test ke marks enter hon aur system automatically bache ki Performance Graph generate kare.
Parents ko auto-SMS jaye ke "Aapke bache ke Physics Grand Test mein 80/100 marks aaye hain".
3. 💵 Teacher Per-Lecture / Percentage Payroll Ledger
Schools mein teacher ki fixed salary hoti hai, lekin coaching mein teacher ko Per Lecture (Hourly) ya Per Student Fee Percentage milti hai.

Ek automated ledger jo khud hisaab lagaye ke Sir Ali ne is mahine 20 lectures diye hain, tou unki payment kitni bani.
Agar percentage basis pe hai, tou "Physics Batch A" ke total revenue ka 40% teacher ke account mein auto-calculate ho jaye.
4. 📚 Digital Library & Past Papers Repository
Bacho ke liye ek Student Portal jahan wo login kar ke:

Apne teachers ke Notes aur Guess Papers download kar sakein.
Pishlay 10 saal ke Past Papers access kar sakein.
Agar koi lecture miss ho gaya hai, tou wahan Recorded Video Lecture dekh sakein.
5. 💳 Installment & Dues Management (For Crash Courses)
Coaching mein crash courses (e.g., 2 months preparation) ki fee ikathi hoti hai jise bache installments mein dete hain.

System track kare ke 15,000 package tha, 5,000 de diye hain, remaining 10,000 ka reminder auto-generate ho.
Kyunke aap ek Coaching Center ka software bana rahay hain, in mein se Batch Management aur Test Series sab se zyada zaroori aur game-changing features sabit honge. Aap batayein, in mein se kis par kaam start karein?
